# Design System — Vinylist / Hi-Fi Analog Minimalist

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Visual World & Aesthetic
- **Metaphor**: Hi-Fi Analog Turntable, Vinyl record sleeve, Editorial Swiss typography, split dual-tone canvas.
- **Palette**:
  - **Dark Canvas (Left / Vinyl Deck)**: `#141619` (Deep Charcoal Black), `#1b1e22` (Deck Surface), `#24282e` (Subtle Inset)
  - **Cream / Warm Linen Canvas (Right / Editorial Sleeve)**: `#e9e4d9` (Warm Bone/Parchment), `#ded8cc` (Card/Parchment Borders), `#f3efe6` (Highlight)
  - **High-Contrast Typography**: `#ffffff` on dark, `#18181b` on cream, `#8b8882` (Muted Warm Taupe)
  - **Accent**: `#e53935` (Analog LED Red / Vinyl Record Label Accent), `#c59b27` (Vintage Gold Brass Tonearm)
  - **Borders & Dividers**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` on dark, `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)` on cream.

## Typography
- **Headings & Brand**: `'Syne', 'Outfit', sans-serif` (Heavy Weights: 700, 800, 900) for "vinylist / SoundSort" and track numbers.
- **Body & Editorial**: `'Plus Jakarta Sans', 'DM Sans', sans-serif` (Weights: 400, 500, 600) for track listings, genres, and metadata.
- **Micro-labels**: Minimum 12px for WCAG compliance.

## Key Visual Elements
1. **Split-Tone Layout**: Left side dark analog turntable deck, right side warm cream vinyl record sleeve & track curation hub.
2. **Interactive Spinning Vinyl Record**: Central vinyl turntable with grooves, tonearm needle, center label with current track artwork, and smooth rotation when active.
3. **Hi-Fi Waveform Equalizer Bar**: Bottom analog player bar with red LED waveform visualizer, play/pause toggle, and transport controls.
4. **Side A / Side B Curated Tracklists**: Editorial typography styling for track lists with clean numeral numbering (`01`, `02`, `03`).
